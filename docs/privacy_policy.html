Privacy Policy – CoachBoard

Last updated: August 2026

CoachBoard is an offline coaching notebook. Athletes, sessions, measurements, payments, and coins stay on this device. We do not upload training records to our servers.

Data stored on device

  • Athletes, sessions, programs, and notes
  • Weight logs you enter (not clinical records)
  • Coins and shop purchases (local record)

Not medical advice

CoachBoard does not diagnose, treat, or recommend medical care.

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing
  • Remote config – https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N249.json (no personal data is sent)
